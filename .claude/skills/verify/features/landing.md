# Landing

The standalone marketing page in `landing/` (Astro, static, served by nginx). It has a hero with a static product mock and two links (`Crea la prima lista`, `Guarda come funziona`), four step cells under "Come funziona", a "Perché Zenbree" block, pricing, a FAQ accordion and a closing composer. Every call to action leads to the app's sign-up or sign-in page, built from `PUBLIC_APP_URL`.

## Sub-features

- `landing-hero` shows the heading "Zenbree trova chi contattare".
- `landing-plan` ("Prova un piano", after the statement) prices a plan as the app does: rows times the per-row price of each required column (email 1, phone 8, AI column 1). Up to 100 credits it says the search starts at once; over 100 it waits for approval; over 500 it adds a second confirmation. Its form sends only the brief to sign-up.
- `landing-steps` shows the four steps in `#come` side by side: a snippet of the product on top, the step text below. They are static, with no tabs and no script.
- `landing-faq` opens one answer at a time. All answers are closed on load.
- `landing-links` points sign-up, sign-in, terms and privacy at the app.
- `landing-app-redirect` sends any path the landing does not have to the same path on the app (308), for links from when the app lived on zenbree.com.
- `landing-composer` sends the closing brief to sign-up as `?brief=`.
- `landing-locale` switches between the Italian page at `/` and the English, Spanish and French pages at `/en/`, `/es/` and `/fr/` with the language menu in the header (top right, before "Accedi"): a button with the flag and the language name opens a list of every locale, flag and endonym, the current one marked `aria-current="page"`. Each item is an `<a hreflang>`. The section ids are in the page's language (`#come`, `#perche`, `#prezzi`, `#faq` on `/`; `#how`, `#why`, `#pricing`, `#faq` on `/en/`), and a language switch keeps the section: from `/en/#pricing` the Italian item leads to `/#prezzi`. `link[rel=alternate][hreflang=x-default]` points to `/en/`.
- `landing-phone` fits a 390 px window without horizontal scroll.
- `landing-menu` is the hamburger sheet below 760 px. It holds the four section links and "Accedi". The theme toggle and the language menu sit in its top right, next to the close button; the header hides them below 760 px.
- `landing-theme` follows `prefers-color-scheme` until the theme toggle stores a choice in `localStorage.theme` (`light` or `dark`; absent means the OS). The toggle is one icon button, "Tema: Scuro" / "Theme: Dark" with `aria-pressed`: a sun in the light scheme, a moon in the dark one. CSS picks the icon (the `dark` variant in `theme.css`), so it is right before any script runs. Its text reaches WCAG AA in both schemes at 1440 and 390 px. The footer has no theme or language control.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open the landing URL. Use the header anchors, the FAQ and the buttons "Prova gratis" and "Accedi".

## Driving it with bin/browser

Preconditions:

- The stack is up (`bin/stack up`), so `$VERIFY_WEB` answers. The landing is not part of `up`.
- `bin/stack compose up -d --build landing` has finished. The page is on `http://localhost:14321`.

- **Hero.** `bin/browser goto http://localhost:14321`. `ax` shows a `heading` that contains "Zenbree trova chi contattare".
- **Plan.** On `/`, `[data-cost]` reads `100 crediti` and `[data-rule]` starts with "Fino a 100". Click the example buttons (`button[data-example]`) in turn: 160 (approval), 540 (approval and second confirmation), back to 100. Set `#plan-rows` to 300 and fire `input`: `600 crediti` (email and AI column, 2 credits a row). At 500 rows with email and phone checked and the AI column unchecked it reads `4.500 crediti`, the figure of the worked example in `#prezzi` (`500 × (1 + 8) = 4.500 crediti al massimo`, then `Starter + ricariche da 3.000 e 1.000 = €227 + IVA`). `[...new FormData(document.querySelector('form[data-estimate]')).keys()]` prints `["brief"]`.
- **Steps.** `bin/browser eval "[document.querySelectorAll('#come ol > li').length, document.querySelectorAll('[role=tab]').length]"` prints `[4, 0]`. Each step has an `h3` with its title (`Descrivi`, `Approva il piano`, `Ricevi la lista`, `Contatta`).
- **FAQ.** `bin/browser eval "document.querySelectorAll('details[name=faq][open]').length"` prints `0` on load. Click the summary of an item (`bin/browser click css 'details[name=faq]:nth-of-type(2) summary'`): the query prints `1`. Click another one: it still prints `1`, and the open item is the one you clicked.
- **Links.** `bin/browser eval "[...document.querySelectorAll('a')].map(a => a.href).filter(h => h.includes('/auth/') || h.endsWith('/terms') || h.endsWith('/privacy'))"` lists only URLs that start with `$VERIFY_WEB`.
- **App redirect.** `curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code} %{redirect_url}' 'http://localhost:14321/lists?x=1'` prints `308 $VERIFY_WEB/lists?x=1`; `/en/` and `/` still answer 200.
- **Composer.**
  1. `bin/browser goto http://localhost:14321`.
  2. `bin/browser fill textbox "Descrivi il tuo cliente ideale" "agenzie a Milano"`, then `bin/browser click button "Inizia gratis"`.
  3. `bin/browser url` is `$VERIFY_WEB/auth/sign-up?brief=agenzie+a+Milano`. The form always goes to sign-up, with or without a session in the app.
- **Locale.**
  1. `bin/browser goto http://localhost:14321`. `bin/browser eval "document.documentElement.lang"` prints `it`.
  2. `bin/browser click css 'header details[data-lang-menu] summary'`. The `details` is `open` and the screenshot shows the list under the button. `bin/browser click css 'h1'` closes it.
  3. Open it again and `bin/browser click css 'header details[data-lang-menu] a[hreflang=en]'`. `bin/browser url` ends with `/en/`, `document.documentElement.lang` prints `en`, the hero `heading` in `ax` contains "Zenbree finds who to contact", and the button reads "English".
  4. Section: on `/en/`, `bin/browser eval "[...document.querySelectorAll('header nav a')].map(a => a.getAttribute('href'))"` prints `["#how", "#why", "#pricing", "#faq"]`. `bin/browser click link "Pricing"`, then pick Italian in the language menu: `location.pathname + location.hash` is `/#prezzi` and the pricing heading is at the top of the window. From `/`, with no hash, the items lead to `/` and `/en/` with no hash.
  5. Keyboard: focus the summary (`document.querySelector('header details[data-lang-menu] summary').focus()`). `press Enter` opens the list. `press ArrowDown` focuses the first item, again the next one, and it wraps; `ArrowUp` goes back. `press Escape` closes the list and the focus is on the summary. From the closed summary, `ArrowDown` opens the list on the first item; `press Enter` on an item follows its link.
- **Phone and middle widths.** `bin/browser eval "document.documentElement.scrollWidth <= window.innerWidth" --width 390 --height 844` prints `true`. Do the same with `--width 800` and `--width 1024`: below 1280 px the hero stacks, below 900 px the step cells go two by two, below 760 px one by one. `viewport 390 844` clamps to 500 px wide, so use `--width`.
- **Menu.** Pass `--width 390 --height 844` to every command, and put a `sleep 1` between commands: the width override ends with each connection, and the next command can start before it is gone. In zsh keep the flags in an array (`W=(--width 390 --height 844)`, `$W`), or they reach the script as one word.
  1. `bin/browser eval "getComputedStyle(document.querySelector('button[popovertarget=site-menu]')).display"` prints `"flex"` at 390 and `"none"` at 1440, where the header `nav` stays `flex`.
  2. `bin/browser click button "Apri il menu"`. `bin/browser eval "document.getElementById('site-menu').matches(':popover-open')"` prints `true`. `bin/browser press Escape` makes it `false`.
  3. Open it again and `bin/browser click link "Prezzi"` (the desktop link is hidden, so there is one match). The popover closes and `location.hash` is `#prezzi`.
- **Theme toggle.** At 1440 px, with nothing stored: `goto` with `--scheme light` and `eval` the `display` of the two `svg` in `header [data-theme-toggle]`: the sun is `block`, the moon `none`, `aria-pressed` is `false`. With `--scheme dark` the moon shows and `aria-pressed` is `true`, still with `scheme-light-dark` on `<html>`. `bin/browser click button "Tema: Scuro" --scheme light`: `<html>` has `scheme-dark`, the background is `rgb(11, 12, 16)`, `localStorage.theme` is `dark` and the moon shows. `goto` again: still dark. `goto /en/`: still dark; click `button "Theme: Dark"`: `scheme-light`, `rgb(250, 251, 253)`, `light`, and with `--scheme dark` it stays light after a reload.
- **Phone sheet.** With `--width 390 --height 844` on every command and a `sleep 1` between: the header toggle and language `details` have `display` `none`. Open the menu: the sheet has no radio, and its toggle, language summary and close button are 44 px tall on the logo's row. Click `#site-menu summary`: the list opens over the links. `press Escape`: the list closes, the sheet stays open and the focus is on the summary; a second `Escape` closes the sheet. Click `#site-menu [data-theme-toggle]`: the theme flips and the header toggle has the same `aria-pressed`.
- **Step cards at 900 to 1000 px.** The four step cells stay in four columns down to 900 px, so their snippet cards are narrow there. A page-level `scrollWidth` check cannot see text that leaves a card. On `/`, `/en/`, `/es/` and `/fr/`, at `--width 905`, `920` and `960`, this prints `0` (the Spanish and French answer chips of step 1 once left their card by up to 36 px): `bin/browser eval "Math.round(Math.max(0, ...[...document.querySelectorAll('#come .shadow-snippet')].flatMap(c => [...c.querySelectorAll('*')].map(e => e.getBoundingClientRect().right - c.getBoundingClientRect().right))))" --width 905 --height 900`.
- **Keyboard.** Use `bin/browser viewport 390 844` for the phone part: it stays between commands and its 500 px minimum is still the phone layout, while `--width` ends with each command and hides the menu button before the next `press`.
  1. Desktop: focus the "Domande" header link, `press Tab`: the focus is on the theme toggle with a solid outline; `press Enter` flips the theme. `press Tab`: the language summary.
  2. Phone: focus the header "Prova gratis" link, `press Tab` (focus on "Apri il menu"), `press Enter` (the popover is open). Each `press Tab` then moves through the logo, the theme toggle, the language summary, "Chiudi il menu", the four section links and "Accedi". `press Escape` closes the menu and the focus goes back to "Apri il menu".
- **Theme across locales.** Click the toggle to dark, then pick English in the language menu. `/en/` has the same background and the moon. Clear the key with `bin/browser eval "localStorage.removeItem('theme')"` when done: the verify Chrome profile keeps it.
- **Theme and contrast.** For each of `light` and `dark`, once at 1440 px and once with `--width 390 --height 844` added to step 2:
  1. `bin/browser goto http://localhost:14321`
  2. `bin/browser contrast --reveal --scheme <scheme>` prints `contrast: <n> text styles, 0 below floor` and exits 0. A failing style prints a `FAIL <ratio> < <floor>` line instead.
  3. `bin/browser screenshot <file.png> --full --scheme dark` for the dark proof.
- **Proof.** Save full-page screenshots in light and dark at 1440 and 390 px, one at 1024 and 800 px, and one with an FAQ answer open. Compare them with the design renders (`docs/design-handoff/prototipo/`).

## Gotchas

- `bin/stack up` does not build or start the landing, and the doctor does not check whether the landing image is current. Its port is `14321` whatever `VERIFY_ID` is (`compose.verify.yml`), so two verify stacks cannot both run it.
- `PUBLIC_APP_URL` is baked in at build time. After you change `VERIFY_WEB_PORT`, rebuild with `--build`, or the links point at the old port.
- Without JavaScript every step shows and every FAQ answer is closed. The scripts are the theme toggle, the language menu (arrow keys, Escape, click outside) and the menu sheet. Without them the language list still opens and closes on its button.
- The stored theme lives in `localStorage.theme` on the landing origin, apart from the app's. Colours are `light-dark()` tokens, so the `scheme-*` class on `<html>` decides, not the media query. `--scheme` only moves the OS side, which the page follows with nothing stored, so a stored `light` or `dark` ignores it. The toggle has no way back to the OS: remove the key by hand. `contrast --reveal` mutates the page, so run `goto` before the next check. `contrast --reveal` also opens both language lists. `contrast` without `--reveal` after opening the menu checks the sheet.
- In zsh never name a loop variable `path`: it is tied to `PATH`, and `bin/browser` then fails with `env: node: No such file or directory`.
- `bin/stack down` removes the landing container with the rest of the verify project.
