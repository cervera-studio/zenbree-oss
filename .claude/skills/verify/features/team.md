# Team

An admin of an organization invites people by email from Settings › Team, changes their role (`Amministratore` or `Membro`) and removes them. The invitee opens `/invite/<id>`, signed in with the invited address, and accepts or declines; an invitation expires after 48 hours. A user in several organizations switches between them from the account menu. Members use lists, chats and campaigns, but not API keys, webhooks or billing: the tabs are hidden, the API answers 403, and a removed member's token is refused on its next call (#308).

## Sub-features

- `team-invite` an admin invites an email as `Membro` or `Amministratore`; the invitation is listed under `Inviti in attesa` with its expiry.
- `team-accept` the invitee accepts on `/invite/<id>`, joins as the invited role, and that organization becomes the active one.
- `team-decline` the invitee declines; the invitation is `rejected` and the link then reads `Invito non valido`.
- `team-expire` an expired invitation reads `Invito non valido` and the accept endpoint answers 400.
- `team-for-me` a signed-in user's pending invitations are listed under `Inviti per te` on their Team page.
- `team-role` an admin changes a member's role; the API applies it to a token minted before the change.
- `team-remove` an admin removes a member; that member's token is refused at once (401 `AUTH.NOT_A_MEMBER`), and their sessions move to their own organization; the senders they connected in that organization are disconnected (`owner_removed`, no token) and unlinked from its clients, their senders elsewhere unchanged (#411). Before the removal the API revokes them at the provider (#471): the provider fake receives the Google revoke (unless the same Google account is a live sender in another organization) and the Unipile delete, and the member stays if that call fails; the project's integrations page then shows the mailbox and LinkedIn as disconnected. Setup by SQL: members, a client, `user_oauth_connections` rows with a token encrypted in the api container (`docker exec <api> python -c "from app.integrations import token_crypto as t; print(t.encrypt('x').value)"`), linked in `project_senders`; `bin/fake-unipile` for the Unipile delete (it answers 404, which counts as done). Driven 2026-10-15 with `VERIFY_ID=471`: a member whose Google account is also a sender in his own organization loses the Unipile account and no Google revoke is sent (`integrations.revoke.refused` absent); one whose Google account is unshared gets the revoke (Google answers 400 to the fake token).
- `team-member-view` a member sees the team read-only, no `API key` / `Crediti` tabs or menu item, a notice on `/settings/api-keys`, and 403 `AUTH.ORG_ADMIN_ONLY` on API keys and webhooks.
- `team-switch` the account menu lists the user's organizations from two up (`CAMBIA TEAM`); choosing one reloads the app in it.
- `team-create` (#676) `button "Menu account"` › `menuitem "Nuovo team"`, `fill textbox "Nome del team"`, `click button "Crea team"`: the app reloads in the new team (a new team's default client has no profile, so it opens `/onboarding`; `button "Salta per ora"`). `CAMBIA TEAM` lists both; `/billing` reads `SALDO ATTUALE 0` (no second signup grant) while the user's own team keeps its balance; `/settings/team` shows the user `Amministratore`. psql: the new `organization.slug` is `<creator id>/<uuid>`, never the one the client sent. The creator is its founder: another admin of the team (Bo, a `member` row by SQL) sees no `Ruolo di Ada Verify` combobox and no remove button on `/settings/team`, and Bo's `update-member-role`/`remove-member` calls answer 400 `FOUNDER`. After 4 created teams the dialog shows `alert` `Hai già creato il numero massimo di team.` and creates nothing (memberships by invite don't count). Driven 2026-10-12 with `VERIFY_ID=676`, `VERIFY_LLM=off`.
- `team-pins` Better Auth's `owner` role and slug changes are refused.
- `team-founder` the user whose own organization it is (slug = their id) stays its admin: another admin sees no role select and no `Rimuovi` on their row, and the endpoints answer 400 `FOUNDER`.
- `team-leave` `/api/auth/organization/leave` is disabled (404).
- `team-throttle` invitation emails are throttled (#323): 3 per address and 50 per organization in 24 hours, counting a new invite, a resend and a re-invite; past it the form reads `Hai mandato troppi inviti: riprova domani.` and nothing is sent or cancelled.
- `team-signup-invite` a new user who opens `/invite/<id>`, follows `Registrati` from sign-in and signs up lands back on the invitation after verifying (#323).
- `team-runs` runs and saved searches are the team's (#323): every member reads them; stopping a run, or pausing, editing, running or deleting a saved search, is for whoever started it or an admin (403 otherwise).
- `team-stream` an open chat stream of a member removed mid-turn ends within about 30 s with `error` `AUTH.NOT_A_MEMBER` and `done` (#323).
- `team-low-credits` a low-credit notice names the organization, and its link (`/billing/switch?org=<id>`) makes it the active one before opening `/billing` (#323).
- `team-delete-member` deleting a member's account (#333, Better Auth's `admin/remove-user`, or the member's own delete from [settings profile](settings-profile.md), #341) keeps the clients, lists, chats and campaigns they created, with no creator; their senders are revoked, disconnected and unlinked, and a campaign of theirs keeps running from the client's other senders.
- `team-campaigns` any member reads and runs a colleague's campaign: it sends from its project's senders (#307, [clients](clients.md)), so there is no owner gate. Rows a user makes while active in a team land in the team's organization, not their own.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `link "Impostazioni"` in the sidebar, then the tab `link "Team"` in `navigation "Impostazioni"`. It opens `/settings/team`.
- The invitation email links `/invite/<id>`. A signed-out visitor goes through sign-in and back to it.
- `button "Menu account"` › the organization's `menuitem "<name>"` under `CAMBIA TEAM`.

## Gotchas

- `wait text` matches an element's whole text: wait for a heading such as `Le tue chiavi API`, not a sentence inside a paragraph.
- `bin/browser` drives one tab: hold the second user's JWT in a shell variable to prove what their token can do after the admin acts. Never write it to the artifacts.
- The account menu items are wrapped in a dropdown: `menuitem "<organization name>"` exists only while the menu is open.
- The invite and accept use Better Auth's own endpoints (`/api/auth/organization/*`); the role and membership checks on the data API are in `api/app/auth/middleware.py`.
- Not driven: delivery of the invitation and verification emails (no Resend key; the templates are unit-tested in `web/lib/email-layout.test.ts`, and `verify-link` rebuilds the link with the callback the sign-up form sends, which `sign-up-form.test.tsx` checks), and billing for members ([billing](billing.md)). Without an LLM key the shared run never starts: the stream proof uses a queued run.

- A new sign-in starts in the user's own organization (`session.create.before`): switch to the team before testing it.
