# Notifications

The bell at the top of the sidebar, beside the logo (in the top bar on phones), opens a panel of in-app notifications (replies, finished or stopped searches, questions from the agent, messages waiting for approval, low credits, failed payments, accepted LinkedIn invitations, bounces, mailboxes in error, plan renewals), grouped by day (#173, #215, #343). Under the header «N da leggere» / «Tutte lette» sit the filter chips «Tutte», «Da leggere», «Risposte» and «Da decidere» with unread counts. A reply shows the sender's initials with a channel badge (email, LinkedIn, or a calendar for a meeting request) and the opening of the reply's text; a system kind shows an icon tile. Each item has a time, an unread dot and, by kind, actions: «Rispondi» + «Apri», «Accetta» (meeting request), «Rivedi e approva», «Riprova». «Segna tutte come lette» is in the footer and the settings icon in the header. Settings › Notifiche chooses the email and browser channel per event, the digest frequency and hour, and do-not-disturb, and saves each change at once (#343). The new kinds (LinkedIn invitation accepted, too many bounces, mailbox in error, plan renewed) show in the panel too.

## Sub-features

- `notif-empty` opens the panel with no notifications: «Sei in pari».
- `notif-kinds` (#215): every kind's icon and subtitle; an item opens what it refers to and is marked read.
- `notif-filter` (#215, #343): the chips «Tutte», «Da leggere», «Risposte», «Da decidere» filter the items and count unread ones; after read-all «Da leggere» shows «Sei in pari».
- `notif-actions` (#343): per-kind action links, the reply quote and the channel badge; keyboard pass through chips and actions.
- `notif-org-switch` (#743): a click on a notification about another organization makes that organization active, then opens the chat, campaign or reply there.
- `notif-unread` shows the unread count on the bell and the item in the panel.
- `notif-read-all` marks every notification read.
- `notif-live` (#242): new items, counts and reads from another tab reach an open tab over SSE, without a reload.
- `notif-settings` changes the per-event channels, the digest frequency and hour and do-not-disturb; each change autosaves, and a failed save reverts.
- `notif-push` (#343): Web Push. The settings card turns it on (`button "Attiva notifiche del browser"`), `button "Invia una prova"` sends a test, and a notification reaches the browser with no Zenbree tab open. After a campaign starts, an inline prompt offers the same.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `button "Notifiche"` at the top of the sidebar. With unread items its name is `button "Notifiche, <n> da leggere"`.
- In the panel, choose `link "Impostazioni notifiche"` (the sliders icon in the header), or open `link "Impostazioni"` then the tab `link "Notifiche"` (`/settings/notifications`).

## Driving it with bin/browser

Preconditions:

- A seeded user, signed in and past onboarding.
- No notifications yet: `bin/stack psql -tAc "SELECT count(*) FROM notifications"` prints `0`.

- **Empty panel.** `bin/browser click button "Notifiche"`. `ax` shows a `dialog` with `heading "Notifiche"`, `paragraph` `Tutte lette`, `group "Filtra notifiche"` with `button "Tutte"`, `button "Da leggere"`, `button "Risposte"` and `button "Da decidere"` (no counts at 0), `button "Chiudi"`, `Sei in pari`, `button "Segna tutte come lette" [disabled]` and `link "Impostazioni notifiche"`. Close it with `button "Chiudi"` or `bin/browser press Escape`.
- **Unread item.** Real events need a chat turn, a reply or a payment. As setup only, insert one: `bin/stack psql -tAc "INSERT INTO notifications (user_id, organization_id, kind, href) SELECT id, default_organization(id), 'low_credits', '/billing' FROM \"user\" WHERE email='<email>'"`. After a reload the bell is `button "Notifiche, 1 da leggere"`; the panel lists `link "Crediti in esaurimento (da leggere) …"` under `region "Oggi"`.
- **Messages to approve (real beat).** As setup, an `active` campaign with `activated_at` an hour ago (watching a campaign you just started notifies nothing), one email step and 12 `active` targets (SQL in the #215 PR). Within a minute the `dispatch_campaign_sends` beat holds 12 messages and the open tab, without a reload, shows `button "Notifiche, 1 da leggere"` and `link "12 messaggi da approvare (da leggere)"`, with the body `<campaign> · partono dopo il tuo ok …` as a separate paragraph (a link's name is only the title and `(da leggere)`). Clicking it lands on `/campaigns/<id>` and the row's `read_at` is set. A second call while it is unread adds nothing.
- **Another organization (`notif-org-switch`, #743).** Needs a user who is a member of two organizations (see [team](team.md)). As setup, seed a chat in organization A (`chat_sessions` with `organization_id` A and the user's `user_id`) and a `search_done` notification for the user with `organization_id` A and `href` `/?s=<chat id>`, then sign in with B active (`GET /api/auth/get-session` shows `activeOrganizationId` B). `GET /v1/notifications` returns `href` `/switch?org=<A>&next=%2F%3Fs%3D<chat id>` (the row keeps `/?s=<chat id>`). Click the item in the bell: the page lands on `/?s=<chat id>` with the chat open (not `Questa chat non esiste più`), `activeOrganizationId` is now A and the `zb-project` cookie is cleared. Opening the panel without a click must not switch: with items of two organizations in the bell (run this on a production build, which the verify stack is, because the router prefetches the visible links only there), `activeOrganizationId` and the `zb-project` cookie are the same after the panel has been open for several seconds, and `/switch` requests with `next-router-prefetch: 1` change nothing. Clicking an item of the active organization changes neither. `/switch?org=<an organization the user is not in>&next=/` opens `/` and leaves the active organization alone; `next=https://evil.test/` and `next=//evil.test` land on `/`.
- **Chat turn kinds.** Without an LLM key no turn runs, so as setup seed `chat_sessions` (with titles) and person/company `lists` with rows, then run the real service in the worker: `bin/stack compose exec -T worker python` with `n.notify_turn(conn, uid, sid, <12 min ago>, n.turn_outcome(...))` then `n.deliver(...)`, for a `needs_input` funnel question, a stage with `error: {code: 'provider'}` (`units 100, done 64`) and a succeeded turn with `refunded 14`. The panel shows `L'agente ha bisogno di te` / `<chat> · nessun risultato`, `Ricerca ferma: un fornitore non risponde` / `<chat> · 64 di 100, le altre non le paghi`, `Ricerca finita: 47 persone in 31 aziende` / `<chat> · 14 crediti resi`. With a tab open and a turn started a minute ago, `notify_turn` returns `None`. A labelled reply goes through `app.outreach.replies.notify(<message id>)`: `Marco Bellini chiede un incontro` / `Richiesta di incontro`, icon `MB`.
- **Filters (#343).** `bin/browser click button "Risposte <n>"`, `button "Da decidere <n>"`, `button "Da leggere <n>"`: each chip is a `button` with `aria-pressed`, its count is the unread items of that group (`Da leggere` is the server's total), and only that group's items stay. After read-all `button "Da leggere"` shows `Sei in pari`. The chips sit in `group "Filtra notifiche"`.
- **Replies seeded for real (#343).** Insert `outreach_inbound_messages` (email and LinkedIn, `label` set, a `project_id`, a `campaign_id`) as setup, then in the worker run `asyncio.run(replies.notify('<message id>'))` (`bin/stack compose exec -T worker python -`). The same script calls `n.notify(conn, uid, kind, href=…, data=…)` then `n.deliver(…)` for `approval_needed`, `search_done`, `search_failed`, `agent_question`, `low_credits`, `payment_failed`. Check `SELECT data ? 'quote' FROM notifications` is `f` for every row: the quote is computed at read time.
- **Actions, quote and badge (#343).** Under `Risposte` each reply shows `«<opening of the text>»`, an avatar badge (`image "Email"`, `image "LinkedIn"`, `image "Incontro"` for a meeting request), `link "Rispondi: <title>"` → `/inbox?m=<id>#reply` and `link "Apri: <title>"` → `/inbox?m=<id>`; a meeting request has only `link "Accetta: <title>"`. A meeting request only counts from a verified sender (#468): a reply whose sender is not verified is kind `reply`, not `reply_urgent`, with `Mittente non verificato` in its subtitle, `image "Email"` and no `Incontro`, and `Rispondi` and `Apri` actions. `Da decidere` shows `link "Rivedi e approva: <title>"` → `/campaigns/<id>`; a stopped search shows `link "Riprova: <title>"`. Clicking an action closes the panel, marks the item read (`read_at` set) and lands on the target. `#reply` is the answer form (`form#reply`) of a thread whose channel has a connected sender; with none the thread shows `Nessuna casella collegata…` and the hash has no target. The seeded replies have no agent draft, so the box is empty.
- **Keyboard (#343).** `bin/browser press Tab` walks the header (settings, close), the chips, then per item the title link and each action, and `bin/browser press Enter` on a focused chip toggles `aria-pressed`. `bin/browser` has no `Space` key: native button Space is not driven. Read the focus with `bin/browser eval "document.activeElement.textContent"`; screenshots show the ring on the item and on the action.
- **Time zone (#479).** With an unread reply item (see `Replies seeded for real`), `bin/browser stop; TZ=Europe/Rome bin/browser start`, sign in again and open the bell: the item's `time` shows the Rome clock of its `created_at`, the same as the home `Risposte per te` card (`Ultima: … · HH:MM`). `bin/browser eval "document.cookie"` contains `NEXT_TIME_ZONE=Europe/Rome`. Before #479 the bell showed the UTC clock.
- **Read all.** `bin/browser click button "Segna tutte come lette"`. `bin/stack psql -tAc "SELECT kind, read_at IS NOT NULL FROM notifications"` prints `low_credits|t`.
- **Settings.** The page autosaves: each change PUTs the whole settings and shows a toast `Salvato`; there is no save button.
  1. `bin/browser click link "Impostazioni notifiche"`, then `bin/browser wait-url /settings/notifications`. `ax` shows a `table` grouped `RISPOSTE`, `CAMPAGNE E RICERCHE`, `ACCOUNT`, with `sempre` and a lock in the `Nell'app` cell of every event (no checkbox: in-app is always on). `Ricerca ferma o serve una tua scelta` sits under `CAMPAGNE E RICERCHE` with email and browser on by default. `Troppi rimbalzi in una campagna` sits there too (browser only, off by default). `Invito LinkedIn accettato` sits under `RISPOSTE` (email and browser off). `ACCOUNT` holds `Crediti sotto 200` (browser only, on), `Casella scollegata o bloccata` and `Ricevute, rinnovi e sicurezza` (both locked, browser `Non disponibile`). A kind that always emails shows `sempre` in the email cell, and a kind with no browser alert shows `Non disponibile`.
  2. `bin/browser click checkbox "Ricerca finita: Email"`. The toast reads `Salvato`.
  3. `curl -H "Authorization: Bearer $T" "$VERIFY_API/v1/notifications/settings"` shows `"search_done":{"email":true,…}`. After a reload, `ax` shows `checkbox "Ricerca finita: Email" [checked]`.
  4. `bin/browser select combobox "Frequenza del riepilogo" "Ogni lunedì"` and `bin/browser select combobox "Ora del riepilogo" "18:00"` (native selects, each autosaves). After a reload both keep their value; the curl body shows `"digest":{"frequency":"weekly","hour":18}` and `bin/stack psql -tAc "SELECT digest_frequency, digest_hour FROM notification_settings"` prints `weekly|18`. `Mai` stores `off` and clears the queued digest items.
  5. `bin/browser click switch "Non disturbare"`, reload: the switch keeps its state and the curl body shows `"dnd":true`.
- **New kinds (#343).** `linkedin_accepted`, `bounces_high`, `mailbox_error` and `plan_renewed` come from the Unipile and Stripe webhooks and the send planner, which need vendor secrets. As setup, run the real `n.notify(conn, uid, kind, href=…, data=…, org_id=…)` then `n.deliver(…)` in `bin/stack compose exec -T worker python -`, and read `GET /v1/notifications`: each item carries its `kind`, `data` and a `browser` flag that follows the settings row. The triggers themselves (transition only, not the user's own action) are proved by `api/tests/test_linkedin_notify_sql.py`, `test_notification_triggers_sql.py` and `test_billing_admin_sql.py`. The panel text for these kinds comes from PR1's panel; check it there.
- **Web Push (`notif-push`, #343).** It needs a VAPID pair and the real push service, so it is driven in headless Chrome with a real FCM subscription. The pair is throw-away: `cd api && uv run python` with `py_vapid.Vapid02().generate_keys()`; the public key is the URL-safe base64 of `public_key.public_bytes(X962, UncompressedPoint)`, the private one the 32-byte `private_numbers().private_value` in URL-safe base64. Export `VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY`, `VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY` and `VAPID_SUBJECT=mailto:verify@zenbree.test` before `bin/stack up` (the overlay passes them to the api, worker and beat). Never commit keys.
  1. **Grant over CDP and keep the connection.** A small Node script connects to the browser endpoint (`http://127.0.0.1:<port>/json/version`, the port is in `$VERIFY_STATE/browser.json`) and sends `Browser.setPermission {permission: {name: 'notifications'}, setting: 'granted' | 'denied', origin: '$VERIFY_WEB'}`, then stays alive; run it in the background and stop it to go back to `default`. `bin/browser eval "Notification.permission"` after a reload prints it.
  2. **Card states.** Reload `/settings/notifications`. `default` or granted without a subscription: `status` `Ricevi le risposte anche con Zenbree chiuso.` and `button "Attiva notifiche del browser"`. `denied`: the warning `Il browser blocca le notifiche di Zenbree.`. After step 3: `Notifiche del browser attive su questo computer.`, `button "Invia una prova"` and `button "Disattiva su questo browser"`.
  3. **Subscribe.** With permission granted, `bin/browser click button "Attiva notifiche del browser"`. The page registers `/sw.js` (served by the web, no sign-in: `curl -sI "$VERIFY_WEB/sw.js"` is 200, `content-type: text/javascript`, `service-worker-allowed: /`), subscribes with the key from `GET /v1/notifications/push` and PUTs it. `bin/stack psql -tAc "SELECT left(endpoint, 40), user_id FROM push_subscriptions"` prints one `https://fcm.googleapis.com/…` row.
  4. **Test.** `bin/browser click button "Invia una prova"`: a toast `Notifica di prova inviata.` and `POST /v1/notifications/push/test` is 200 in `bin/stack logs api`, which means the API's real `pywebpush` request got a 2xx from FCM. The service worker target (`/json/list`, `type: service_worker`) then answers `self.registration.getNotifications()` through `Runtime.evaluate` (after `Target.attachToTarget {flatten: true}`) with a notification titled `Notifiche attive`. The alert follows `"user".locale` (#436): after `bin/browser locale en` and a reload (the bell saves `en`), `button "Send a test"` shows `Notifications on` / `This is a test alert from Zenbree.` The worker target is stopped after about 30 s idle; a push starts it again.
  5. **Closed tab.** `bin/browser goto about:blank` (no Zenbree page left in `/json/list`). As setup, in the worker (`bin/stack compose exec -T worker python -`) run `n.notify(conn, uid, 'reply_urgent', href='/inbox?m=<uuid>', data={'name': 'Marco Bellini', 'label': 'meeting_request', 'channel': 'email', 'campaign': 'Agenzie Milano'})` inside `with acting_as('<org id>', uid), get_conn() as conn:`, print `notified.item['browser']` (`True`), then `n.deliver(notified)`. Within seconds `getNotifications()` in the worker target lists `Risposta interessata` / `Marco Bellini ha risposto alla campagna «Agenzie Milano».`; for an `en` user, `notified.locale` is `en` and the alert is `Interested reply` / `Marco Bellini replied to the campaign "Agenzie Milano".` with `tag` = the notification id and `data.href` = the item's path. No reply text is in it.

To see which window the handler asks for, replace `self.clients` in the worker target with an object whose `openWindow` records its argument, then dispatch `new NotificationEvent('notificationclick', {notification})` for a shown notification: the item's path, and `/` for a notification whose `data.href` is `https://evil.test/x` or `/\t/evil.test/x` (a tab after the slash). Report that the real click was not driven.
  7. **Bell without in-page alert.** Open `/campaigns` with permission granted and the subscription saved, run `window.__shown = []` and wrap `Notification` as in the stub above, deliver a notification as in step 5: the bell goes to `Notifiche, <n> da leggere` and `window.__shown` stays `[]`. After `bin/stack psql -tAc "DELETE FROM push_subscriptions"` (setup) a new one fills `__shown` (`Marco Bellini chiede un incontro`).
  8. **Turn off, then sign out.** `bin/browser click button "Disattiva su questo browser"`: a toast `Notifiche del browser disattivate su questo computer.`, the `push_subscriptions` row is gone, the card is the ask card again, and a reload of any app page does not bring the row back (`localStorage` key `zb-push-off` is `1`; a delivered notification shows no in-page alert either). Click `Attiva notifiche del browser` again (the row is back, the key is gone), then sign out from the account menu: `DELETE /v1/notifications/push` is 200 in `bin/stack logs api` before the session ends, the row is gone, and `bin/browser eval "navigator.serviceWorker.getRegistration('/sw.js').then((r) => r.pushManager.getSubscription())"` is `null`.
  9. **Re-sync on load.** Sign in again with permission still granted. After a restart with a new VAPID pair, the next load unsubscribes the old subscription and PUTs a new endpoint.
  10. **Campaign prompt.** With permission `default`, as setup insert an `active` campaign (`INSERT INTO outreach_campaigns (owner_id, name, status, activated_at, organization_id, project_id) SELECT u.id, 'Verify push', 'active', now(), default_organization(u.id), (SELECT id FROM projects WHERE organization_id = default_organization(u.id) LIMIT 1) FROM "user" u WHERE email = '<email>'`) and open `/campaigns/<id>`: `section "Vuoi un avviso quando qualcuno risponde?"` with `button "Attiva gli avvisi"` and `button "Non ora"`. `Non ora` hides it, also after a reload (`localStorage` key `zb-push-prompt-dismissed`); it is not shown on a draft, with permission granted or denied, or without VAPID keys. The page needs a real `default` permission: stop any CDP grant script first. - **Live push.** Keep the app open. The wallet and the task are per organization (#306): `<org>` is `SELECT organization_id FROM user_credits WHERE user_id = '<id>'`. As setup, `bin/stack psql -tAc "UPDATE user_credits SET credits_remaining = 300 WHERE organization_id = '<org>'"`, run the real balance task (arms the rule; a direct SQL update queues nothing; a user id returns `notified: False`), set the credits to `120`, run it again. The task runs as the organization that queued it, so queue it in that scope. `celery call on_balance_changed` sends no scope and fails with `ScopeMissing`. Queue it with the block below. `bin/stack logs worker` shows `on_balance_changed[…] succeeded` with `'notified': True` on the second run. from app.worker import on_balance_changed
```sh
with queued_for('<org>', 'verify'):
    on_balance_changed.delay('<org>')
EOF
```

### Emails

Every service email (#221) uses one layout: preheader, one title, one primary button (and, in the API ones, optional stat tiles and a secondary button, #351), the reason you got it, and a text part. The API renders the notification ones (`api/app/notifications/layout.py`: urgent reply with a second button `Vedi la bozza`, search done, too many bounces, mailbox disconnected, low credits, failed payment, digest); the web renders verification and password reset (`web/lib/email-layout.ts`) and sends them through Resend. The stack has no `RESEND_API_KEY`, so nothing is sent: render them instead and look at the result.

- **Auth callbacks reach the sender.** Sign up in the browser and request a reset (see [auth](auth.md)); `bin/stack logs web` shows `auth.email.failed` for `verify-email` and `reset-password` with `RESEND_API_KEY or EMAIL_FROM not set`.
- **API emails.** `bin/stack compose exec -T api python -` with a script that prints `n._render(kind, href, data, 'it').__dict__` (`from app.notifications import service as n`) for `reply_urgent` (`{'name', 'label', 'channel', 'campaign'}`; the fourth argument is the language, e.g. `'it'`), `bounces_high` (`{'campaign', 'invalid', 'attempted'}`), `mailbox_error` (`{'mailbox', 'provider'}`; with `'provider': 'unipile'` the email names a LinkedIn account, not a mailbox) and `payment_failed`. The digest is `n.send_digest(n.Digest(email, name, 'daily', {'reply': 1}, 12, [('Acme', 340)], now, 'it'))` with `n.send_email` replaced by a stub that keeps the html: it lists the approvals, the queued counts and the balance.
- **Search finished and low credits (#351)** carry real data, so produce the item first and render its `data`, in `it` and `en` (`n._render(kind, href, data, 'en')`). Search finished: turn `search_done` email on for the dev user (`n.save_prefs`), then in `bin/stack compose exec -T worker python -` run `n.notify_turn(conn, uid, '<seeded session «Trova CEO di agenzie marketing a Milano»>', <12 min ago>, n.turn_outcome('succeeded', [], None, True, 14, 298, 3))`: `data` gains `list`, `list_id`, `people_list_id` and `balance` from the database (the refund, spend and undelivered numbers are the turn's, passed in because no turn runs without an LLM key). Low credits: as setup insert an `active` `subscriptions` row (`plan_id 'starter'`, `current_period_end` a few weeks out) for the organization, then drive the real balance task as in **Live push** (300, then 180): the row's `data` gains `plan` and `renews_at`. The stack sells nothing (no Stripe price ids), so render with `compose exec -e 'BILLING_PLANS=[…"stripe_price_id":"price_x"…]' -e 'BILLING_PACKS=[…]'` to see the «Passa a Pro» button and the pack line; without them both are absent. Expect `~90 email trovate` / `oppure 18 telefoni` at 180 credits (the `enrich_email` / `enrich_phone` costs) and `Piano Starter` · `2.000 crediti il 1 nov`.
- **Auth emails.** From `web/`, with dummy `RESEND_API_KEY=re_dummy EMAIL_FROM=x@verify.local` and `globalThis.fetch` replaced by a stub that keeps the request body (nothing leaves the machine): `node --experimental-strip-types --input-type=module -e "import { sendAuthEmail } from './lib/email-layout.ts'; …"` for `verify-email` and `reset-password`, then read `html`/`text` from the captured JSON body.
- **Language (#377).** Every email, and since #436 every Web Push alert (see `notif-push` steps 4 and 5), follows `"user".locale` (`en`, `it`, `es` or `fr`; default `en`, #699). Headless Chrome follows the host's appearance, so on a dark Mac the shot shows the dark styles; for light, rename `prefers-color-scheme: dark` in a copy. `bin/browser viewport 390 844` checks the phone width.

## Gotchas

- A worker script runs each service call in a scope, as its real caller does: `with acting_as('<org id>', '<user id>'), get_conn() as conn:` for a user's own notification, `acting_for('<org id>', '<reason>')` for an organization task such as `replies.notify` (`from app.core.scope import acting_as, acting_for`). With no scope a checkout raises `ScopeMissing`.
- The seeded `low_credits` row has no `data`, so the panel reads `Ti restano 0 crediti.` That text is an artifact of the setup row, not a bug.
- A row inserted with SQL is never pushed (only `deliver()` writes the stream): reload to see it, or use the beat task above.
- The browser channel fires from the `notification` frame.
