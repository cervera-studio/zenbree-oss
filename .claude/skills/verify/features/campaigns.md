# Campaigns

A user turns a person list into an outreach campaign on Campagne (`/campaigns`). The index (C1) has status tabs with counts, a table (campaign with its list and people, status, per-step channel chips, a progress bar of the sequence steps done over planned with sent and failures underneath, and the reply rate over the people contacted) and a banner for the messages waiting for approval. With no campaign it shows the C1b checklist. «Nuova campagna da una lista» opens a dialog that creates a draft from a list, and the builder at `/campaigns/<id>` holds the sender mailboxes, up to 12 steps (email or LinkedIn), a preview rendered per person, and the draft's delete and activate buttons. Nothing is sent until the campaign is activated, and every message then waits in an approval queue.

## Sub-features

- `camp-index` lists the user's campaigns with tabs, chips, progress and the approval banner, or the C1b checklist (list, mailbox, LinkedIn) when there are none.
- `camp-create` creates a draft from a person list and opens its builder.
- `camp-steps` adds, edits, saves and removes steps; a removal offers «Annulla». Variable chips insert at the cursor.
- `camp-preview` renders the open step, as typed and unsaved, for a person picked among the first 10, with a reason when a step cannot be sent.
- `camp-fields` (#933) `+ Campo` under a step adds a list column or a signal part as a campaign field and writes its token at the cursor. The `Campi` panel sets each field to required or to a fallback text, and the preview names the fields a person has no value for.
- `camp-snippet` (#933) `Aggiungi uno snippet AI` in the `Campo` menu writes an AI snippet column on the campaign's list for its people, from the list's AI research columns, after the user approves the estimate. The new column is then a field in the menu.
- `camp-senders` (#935) lists each Google and Microsoft account of the client by id, ticked when it is in the pool, with its state badge, today's usage and cap, and a reconnect link when it needs one. The live campaign's `Cambia` dialog uses the same picker.
- `camp-rename` renames the campaign inline.
- `camp-activate` is disabled, with the reasons in the alert banner under the head, until the campaign can start (C2b); with unsaved edits it saves, then activates.
- `camp-delete` deletes a draft after a confirm dialog.
- `camp-detail` (C3) is the page of a campaign that has left draft: header meta, KPI strip, the approval queue and the people table, with the sequence and the senders beside them on desktop.
- `camp-approvals` approves, edits or skips the messages waiting for approval; on a phone (M4) one at a time with fixed actions and an edit sheet. `/campaigns/<id>/approvals` (#864) is the same one at a time on desktop, with «Prossimi» beside it and the letter shortcuts A, S and E. Each card says when the message could go and from where (#346); `Modifica` works for email, LinkedIn invites (note, 0..300) and LinkedIn messages (1..4000).
- `camp-preview-footer` (#346) the preview shows the mailbox sign-off and the opt-out footer under an email step's body, none under a LinkedIn step.
- `camp-source-list` (#346) the builder header links to the source list; `link "Riscrivi con l'agente"` opens the list's chat with a prompt in the composer.
- `camp-replies-link` (#346) `link "Risposte"` on the detail page, with an unread badge, opens `/inbox?campaign=<id>`.
- `camp-hold` (#931) each person's row says what holds them in Instantly (`Email da Instantly`, `Stop in corso in Instantly`, `Stop non riuscito in Instantly`, `Stop confermato in Instantly`), and an approval that waits for a held person stays off the review and is counted.
- `camp-parallel` (#931) `switch "Ammetti persone già in un’altra campagna attiva"` in the builder and on the campaign page sets `block_active_duplicates`.
- `camp-variants` (#934) an A/B test of the copy: a step's `b` copy, the share of B, each person's variant, B's copy in the preview and in the sends. The builder writes copy B, previews it and sets the share; a live campaign pauses the allocation and tags people and approvals.
- `camp-pause-reply` (#346) an auto-reply pauses the person, and the beat resumes them at `paused_until`.
- `camp-mailbox-replies` (#1165) a reply read from the sending mailbox: the person it answers is `replied`, it reaches the Inbox, an opt-out in their words excludes them on a passing DMARC verdict, an unrelated message is never fetched whole or stored, and the same push delivered again records nothing.
- `camp-bounces` (#747) a bounce read from the sending mailbox: a hard one stops and excludes the person and gives back a verified address's credit, more than 5 % of 20 sent emails pauses the campaign; a mailbox connected before the read permission asks for a reconnect.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `link "Campagne"` in the sidebar. It opens `/campaigns`.
- On a person list, `link "Avvia campagna"` opens `/campaigns?list=<id>`: the new-campaign dialog is open with that list preselected.
- Each campaign in the index links to its builder (C2, #217: accordion steps with «Giorno N», sender pool with caps and warm-up, read-only window and approval, live preview per step and person, inline rename). The topbar path links back with `link "Campagne"` (it names the campaign last).
