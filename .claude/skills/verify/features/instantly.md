# Instantly

A user connects an Instantly workspace on Settings › Integrazioni, then adds the checked people of a person list to one of its campaigns. The flow never starts, pauses or edits a campaign: the user picks the campaign, maps the list columns to Instantly's fields, previews who goes and who does not (and why), and confirms. A running campaign needs a second, explicit consent. The results page shows each person's outcome and a link to the campaign in Instantly. Every email is checked again just before it goes.

## Sub-features

- `instantly-connect` validates a key against Instantly, stores it encrypted and shows `Connesso`; a wrong key shows an actionable error and stores nothing.
- `instantly-disconnect` clears the key and keeps the record of the people added.
- `instantly-pick-map` picks a campaign (`In corso` for a running one) and maps the list columns.
- `instantly-preview` shows how many people go, each reason that leaves some out, and the sending warning with its checkbox.
- `instantly-send` confirms, follows the batch and shows each person's outcome with the campaign link.
- `instantly-uncertain` shows a person as `Non certe` after a timeout, and the retry that checks Instantly first.
- `instantly-cancel-retry` cancels a live batch and retries a failed one.
- `instantly-latest-batch` finds a batch again after the dialog closes: on the Instantly card, and by the dialog opening on it, with `Aggiungi altre persone`.
- `instantly-reconcile-retry` retries a cancelled batch whose people are `Non certe`: it only looks in Instantly, and never sends.
- `instantly-phone` runs the same flow on a phone width.
- `instantly-stops` (#931) a native reply or opt-out of a person Instantly holds queues a stop: the person reads `Stop in corso in Instantly` on their native campaign, the card lists the stop until Instantly confirms it, a failed stop offers `Riprova` and `Ho rimosso la persona in Instantly`, and `Stop confermato in Instantly` shows only once it is confirmed.
- `instantly-events-hubspot` (#932) logs those events on the contact's HubSpot timeline for the user who sent the person, and the HubSpot card shows what is logged, waiting, failed or unsure. It is driven in [integrations](integrations.md) (*Timeline of external activity*), with this stand-in as the Instantly side.
- `instantly-events` (#930) records the campaign events of the held leads once, from the webhook and from the beat's reads, and the card says how they arrive (`live`, `polled`, `paused`), why there is no webhook and how fresh the record is.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `link "Impostazioni"`, then the tab `link "Integrazioni"`: `region "Instantly"` holds the connect form (`textbox "Nome"`, `textbox "Chiave API"`, `button "Connetti"`) or, once connected, `button "Disconnetti"`.
- On a person list (`/lists/<id>`), check rows: the bar offers `button "Aggiungi a Instantly"` (on a phone it is in the sticky bar). Company lists do not offer it.

## Driving it with bin/browser

Preconditions:

- No real Instantly workspace and no real email. Start the stand-in before `bin/stack up` and export its base URL in the same shell: `bin/fake-instantly > instantly.log &`, then `export INSTANTLY_API_BASE_URL=http://host.docker.internal:18768`. Without it the API calls api.instantly.ai and every connect fails with a refused key. The stand-in's key is `verify-instantly-key`; it keeps its leads in memory, `curl localhost:18768/state` lists them and every add call, and `curl -X POST localhost:18768/reset` empties both. No Resend or Unipile key may be set, and the stack has no mailbox: nothing in this flow sends an email, and the proof that it never does is `SELECT count(*) FROM outreach_campaign_sends` staying 0.
- Sign in as `dev@zenbree.local` (the dev seed's person list `Agenzie marketing Milano · SDR`). Make one person's email reach the stand-in's timeout path: `bin/stack psql -tAc "UPDATE list_rows SET cells = jsonb_set(cells, '{email}', '\"timeout@verify.test\"') WHERE seq = 3 AND list_id = (SELECT id FROM lists WHERE name = 'Agenzie marketing Milano · SDR')"` (Chiara Gallo). The seed's list ids are random: look a list up by name.
- Only the user who connects it, or an admin, may disconnect.

## Gotchas

- The stand-in is the only Instantly: never export a real key, and never point `INSTANTLY_API_BASE_URL` at api.instantly.ai for a run. The stack overlay passes the variable through, and the default is the real host, so forgetting the export sends the connect to the real API with a fake key (refused with 401, no data leaves).
- Instantly can take seconds to index a new lead, so a retry within a minute of a timeout is refused on purpose.
- The campaign link pattern (`https://app.instantly.ai/app/campaign/<id>`) is not in Instantly's API reference. Check it by hand in a real account before relying on it.
- A 403 for a missing scope maps to `INSTANTLY.MISSING_SCOPE`; Instantly's documented codes do not say that a missing scope is a 403, so the stand-in does not cover it.
- The workspace's mailboxes on Settings › Mittenti (#936, `GET /api/v2/accounts`, `POST /plan {"accounts":false}`) are driven in `features/senders.md` (Instantly mailboxes).
- Not driven: a real workspace, a real plan error (402), throttling with a real `Retry-After`, a worker killed in the middle of a chunk (the SQL tests cover it), and the real webhook delivery of Instantly (the stand-in posts the payload its API reference documents).
- Driven end to end 2026-10-16 at d4dfa65 (PR #957). Not driven: a cancel that lands while a chunk is in flight (a list holds fewer than the 100 people of a second chunk), and the native claim of a held person at send time (it needs an active campaign with a mailbox; `test_dispatcher_sql.py` covers it).
- Driven again 2026-10-19 at dbe82cf6 (#1032) in Italian at 1280 px after the copy moved to "add": connect, the dialog (campaign, mapping, preview with a skip reason, add, cancel, `Aggiungi altre persone`), the `Ultima aggiunta a Instantly` card and its cancel, the disconnect dialog, the Instantly mailboxes on Mittenti and the kill-switch dialog. Not driven: the empty mailbox state (the stand-in list is fixed), `en`/`es`/`fr` and 375 px.
