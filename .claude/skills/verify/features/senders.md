# Senders

A signed-in user sees every connected sending mailbox (Gmail or Outlook) on Settings › Mittenti. Each card shows a status pill, a pause or reconnect action, today's sends against the daily cap, why it cannot send now and when it resumes, and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC state of the sender's domain.

## Sub-features

- `senders-list` shows one card per mailbox with its label, provider, status pill and sends progress bar.
- `senders-state` (#935) names each mailbox's state in the pill (`Pronta`, or the reason: `In pausa`, `Da ricollegare`, `Limitata`, `Limite giornaliero raggiunto`, `Fuori orario di invio`, `Invii distanziati`, `Disattivata`) and, when it cannot send, a line saying why and `Riprende a inviare da <date>` when the time is known.
- `senders-pause` pauses and resumes a mailbox by its id (`PATCH /v1/integrations/senders/<id>`) with `button "Metti in pausa <address>"` and `button "Riattiva <address>"`.
- `senders-reconnect` shows `Da ricollegare` and `link "Ricollega"` instead of the pause button when the connection is in error.
- `senders-dns` shows the SPF, DKIM and DMARC chips of the domain.
- `senders-window` (#348) sets each mailbox's send window: the hours and the weekdays when its campaigns send.
- `senders-accounts` (#327): `region "Account dell'organizzazione"` lists every live mailbox and LinkedIn account of the organization, `Usato da: <clients>` (or `Non usato da nessun cliente`) and `collegato da <name>`. Whoever connected one, or an admin, gets `button "Usa in un altro cliente"`: a dialog `Clienti che usano <account>` with one `switch "<client>"` per client. On links it there: a mailbox joins the client's other mailboxes (#935), and a LinkedIn account replaces the client's one (a colleague's live one is refused, `<client> usa già l’account LinkedIn di un collega…`). Off unlinks it; the last unlink leaves it connected and listed. A member sees the list without the button; `PUT|DELETE /v1/integrations/senders/accounts/<id>/projects/<client>` is 403 `INTEGRATIONS.LINK_FORBIDDEN` for them.

- `senders-instantly` (#936): with Instantly connected, `region "Gestite da Instantly"` lists the workspace's mailboxes (`list "Caselle di Instantly"`), each with Instantly's status and warm-up tags and `Limite giornaliero in Instantly: <n>` (or `non impostato`), and no control. A native card whose address Instantly also has shows the tag `Gestita da Instantly` and `Instantly invia anche da questa casella…`. A key without `accounts:read` shows one line with `link "Integrazioni"` instead of the section; not connected shows nothing. `GET /v1/integrations/instantly/accounts` answers `{state, accounts}`.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `link "Impostazioni"` in the sidebar, then the tab `link "Mittenti"`. It opens `/settings/senders`.
- `link "Collega una casella"` in the page header leads to `/settings/integrations`.

## Driving it with bin/browser

Preconditions:

- Seed and sign in a user past onboarding, as in the README.
- A mailbox cannot be connected without Google or Microsoft OAuth keys, so seed rows (`<uid>` is the user's id):
  `bin/stack psql -tAc "WITH s AS (INSERT INTO user_oauth_connections (organization_id,user_id,provider,refresh_token_encrypted,account_label,status) SELECT default_organization('<uid>'),'<uid>',p,'x',l,st FROM (VALUES ('google','ale@ritmosales.it','connected'),('microsoft','marco@ritmosales.it','error')) v(p,l,st) RETURNING id,provider,organization_id) INSERT INTO project_senders (project_id,sender_id,provider,organization_id) SELECT (SELECT id FROM projects WHERE organization_id = default_organization('<uid>') ORDER BY created_at LIMIT 1),id,provider,organization_id FROM s"`

- **Open.** `bin/browser click link "Impostazioni"`, `bin/browser click link "Mittenti"`, then `bin/browser wait heading "Mittenti"`. `ax` shows two level-3 headings named by the mailbox address, each with `progressbar "Invii di oggi"`. The connected card's pill reads `Pronta` inside its sending hours (the seed has Monday to Friday, 9 to 18 in Rome) and `Fuori orario di invio` outside them; both cards read `Inviate oggi: 0 su 10` and `Il limite giornaliero cresce nelle prime due settimane.` (a seeded mailbox is in its first week).
- **Pause.** `bin/browser click button "Metti in pausa ale@ritmosales.it"`, then `bin/browser wait button "Riattiva ale@ritmosales.it"` (`wait text "In pausa"` also matches the toast `Casella in pausa: …`). The button now reads `Riattiva ale@ritmosales.it` (visible text `Riattiva`), and psql `SELECT provider, paused FROM user_oauth_connections` shows `google|t`. Reload to see it persist.
- **Resume.** `bin/browser click button "Riattiva ale@ritmosales.it"`. psql shows `google|f`.
- **Reconnect.** The Microsoft card shows `Da ricollegare` and `link "Ricollega"` (to `/settings/integrations`), and no pause button.
- **Send window.** The connected card reads `Orari di invio (Europe/Rome)` with `spinbutton "Dalle"` (9), `spinbutton "Alle"` (18) and `group "Giorni"` of toggle buttons `lun` … `dom` (`aria-pressed`; `lun` to `ven` on by default). Set `Dalle` to 8 and `Alle` to 20, press `button "sab"`, then `bin/browser click button "Salva orari"`: the toast reads `Orari di invio salvati.` and `bin/stack psql -tAc "SELECT send_window_start_hour, send_window_end_hour, send_window_days FROM user_oauth_connections WHERE account_label='ale@ritmosales.it'"` prints `8|20|{0,1,2,3,4,5}` (0 is Monday). `Salva orari` is disabled while `Dalle` is not before `Alle`. The API is `PUT /v1/integrations/senders/{sender_id}/window`; it changes that row only.
- **State.** Open the hours first, so the clock does not decide: `bin/stack psql -tAc "UPDATE user_oauth_connections SET send_window_start_hour=0, send_window_end_hour=24, send_window_days='{0,1,2,3,4,5,6}'"`, then reload. Both cards read their state from the row: Google `Pronta`, Microsoft (`status='error'`) `Da ricollegare` with `Il fornitore ha revocato l’accesso…`. Set Google `status='error'`: `Da ricollegare`; back to `connected`: `Pronta`. Set `last_used_at = now() + interval '1 hour'`: `Limitata`, the line `Il fornitore ha limitato questa casella…` and `Riprende a inviare da <date and time>` about an hour ahead; `GET /v1/integrations/senders?dns=false` returns `state: held`, `reason: throttled` and `resumes_at`. `last_used_at = now()` reads `Invii distanziati` with a resume time under 14 minutes ahead. Pause Google by id from the card, then `SELECT account_label, paused FROM user_oauth_connections` shows `ale@ritmosales.it|t` and the other row unchanged (also for a second client that uses the account).
- **Proof.** Save `bin/browser ax --out "$VERIFY_ARTIFACTS/senders/list.ax.txt"` and the psql rows.
- **Use in another client.** With a second client (`POST /v1/projects {"name":"Cliente Beta"}`) and the seeded mailbox given a `provider_account_id`: `bin/browser click button "Usa in un altro cliente"`, `bin/browser click switch "Cliente Beta"`, `bin/browser wait text "Ora Cliente Beta usa questo account."`. psql `SELECT p.name FROM project_senders ps JOIN projects p ON p.id = ps.project_id` lists both clients. Switch to `Cliente Beta` (a new client opens `/onboarding`: `Salta per ora`): its Mittenti shows the mailbox, and `GET /v1/integrations/senders?dns=false` (the campaign builder's pool) with `X-Project-Id: <Beta>` returns it. Turn both switches off: `bin/browser wait text "Non usato da nessun cliente · collegato da <name>"` (one node with the whole line; `Non usato da nessun cliente` alone does not match), and psql shows `status = 'connected'` with the token kept and no link; turning one back on links it again.

- **Legacy row removed while its account is already linked (#1099).** In the disposable stack, seed a legacy mailbox L (`provider_account_id IS NULL`) owned by a colleague and an identified account E of the same provider/address owned by the signed-in admin; link both to one client. Both rows `status='connected'` with a non-empty `refresh_token_encrypted` (the driver stubs only the token exchange), `granted_scopes` holding the Gmail send scope, and open sending hours. Run the scenario in the default client: the list and campaign go to the client named by the upload's `project_id` form field or the default one, and `X-Project-Id` is not read by `lists/upload` or campaign create. Pin a two-step conversation to L with its first step sent, and remove E from the campaign pool. Activate the campaign with `POST /v1/campaigns/<id>/activate` (draft targets cannot be set active in SQL). To stage the uncertain send, run the driver once so that `plan` creates the step 2 row, then `UPDATE outreach_campaign_sends SET status='sending', sender_id=<L>, sender_provider='google', attempts=1 WHERE idempotency_key='<campaign>:<target>:2'`. Unlink L through the account dialog (the two `button "Usa in un altro cliente"` share one name: click L's with `--nth <n>`; turn off its client switch, then `bin/browser wait text "Non usato da nessun cliente · collegato da Colleague"`: the card renders that line as one node, so a shorter text does not match): psql must show its target on E with the same `sender_pinned_at`, E in the campaign pool, and the old send still on L. Drive the next step with production `plan` and `send_due` and a stubbed mailbox, `features/senders-legacy-unlink.py`: `bin/stack compose stop beat` first, so that only the script plans and sends; open the sending hours (see State above); then `bin/stack compose cp features/senders-legacy-unlink.py worker:/tmp/senders-legacy-unlink.py` and `bin/stack compose exec -T -w /srv -e PYTHONPATH=/srv worker python /tmp/senders-legacy-unlink.py <org id>` (add the `days` argument, for example `4`, to move the clock past the step 2 delay; run it again for a following pass). It prints `{plan, sent, messages, sends}` (each message with its `In-Reply-To` and `References`, to read the threading back): the next step goes out from E (`messages[].from` is E's address, `sends[].sender_id` is E's id for new rows, the old `sending` row stays on L). A pin is final, so each rerun of the scenario needs a new campaign, and an earlier active campaign with the same recipient emails blocks `plan` (stop or complete it first). Repeat with an admin member release, direct membership removal, and user deletion. Include a target with a `sending` email: its old send remains `sending` on L and no repeat goes out. A different address, provider or organization must inherit nothing; a replied target must be answerable from E. Save the before/after target, pool and send rows.
- **Instantly mailboxes.** Start `bin/fake-instantly` and export `INSTANTLY_API_BASE_URL` before `bin/stack up`, as in `features/instantly.md`, then connect Instantly (key `verify-instantly-key`) in Integrazioni. ## Gotchas

- The seeded `refresh_token_encrypted` is a placeholder: nothing is sent and no token refresh works.
- DNS chips depend on live lookups of the seeded domain (3 s timeout each). In a sandbox they can read `Mancante` or `Sconosciuto`; assert the chips exist, not their grade.
- A new mailbox has a low daily limit that rises over two weeks: the card shows the limit and `Il limite giornaliero cresce nelle prime due settimane.`; the UI never calls it warm-up (#936).
